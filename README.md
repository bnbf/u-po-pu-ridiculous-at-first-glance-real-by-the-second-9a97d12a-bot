A make.fun entry for **Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second**: one sentence, 200 characters at most, pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

## The pitch

> We deliver divorce papers by homing pigeon, because spouses pay process servers $150 a visit to chase exes who never answer the door — and a pigeon can't be dodged.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the single sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
